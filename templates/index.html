Train a model

Pulls each ticker's quarterly financials from its SEC filings (margins, growth, valuation, balance sheet, cash flow, trend vs. its own history) plus price momentum, and pairs them with how the stock did relative to {{ benchmark }} after the release: 1 day, then 1 week, 1 month and 1 year from the first post-release close. Each horizon gets its own model, scored walk-forward on years it never saw.

Comma, space or newline separated. — a few hundred companies across every sector, excluding {{ holdout_size }} tickers kept aside for out-of-sample batch tests. The first run downloads a lot from EDGAR and takes a while; later runs use the cache. Or — the starter universe plus other NYSE/Nasdaq companies, never ones reserved for batch tests.
